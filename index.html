01 — ABOUT MURATKONYA · LONDON

A musician's path,
from reed to resonance.

Murat Ferhat Yegül is a ney player, maker and teacher whose musical life has also included trombone, composition and collaboration.

He graduated from the ney department of the conservatory in Konya in 2005. He later taught at Erciyes University and has taught ney at SOAS and the Yunus Emre Institute in London.

Earlier in his musical life, Murat played trombone and contributed to recordings by Gevende and Kırıka. Album credits list him as a trombonist on Gevende's Ev and Kırıka's Yılların Ettiğini.

Murat also says he gave compositions to groups including Gevende and Kırıka, which performed his work on their albums. On Gevende's Ev, “Şeker” is credited to Ferhat Yeğül as composer.

02 — THE INSTRUMENTA FLUTE OF BREATH

Meet the ney.

An end-blown reed flute with an important place in Turkish classical music and the Mevlevi musical tradition.

The ney’s form is spare; its voice develops through reed, breath, careful making and practice. Its sound asks the player to listen closely to the instrument and to the music around it. Murat will add his own explanations and photographs here.
